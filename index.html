What gets through the filter. A role appears here only if the posting asks for no more than 2 years, the job is in Bengaluru (remote-only is excluded even when the company is based here), and it is full-time rather than an internship. The years figure is read from the posting text, not just the listing tag — several roles that looked junior on a listing page turned out to want four or five years, and those are dropped.
How the refresh works. A GitHub Action runs every morning at 06:00 IST, pulls the Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job feeds plus the Wellfound listings, applies the rules above, and commits the result. Refresh pulls the newest committed list — it does not scrape live, because a page served from GitHub Pages is not allowed to fetch other sites directly. Roles that appeared in the most recent run are badged new.
Marking a role applied saves it in this browser only — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use Export on the applications page to keep a copy, and re-import it if you switch browser or machine.
